All About Fruits

So Many Kinds of Fruits

Fruits come in all different shapes and sizes, and they all have their own textures and flavors. Some can be sweet, while others are sour or even bitter. My favorite thing about fruits is that in different parts of the world, you can find all kinds of them! Not only that, but fruits come in many colors and even mixtures of them.

With fruits, you never know what to expect. For example, the fruit called tamarind grows on trees. On the outside, it has a hard, brown shell with a smooth texture. Inside, it contains little beads. When you open it, the fruit is dark brown and can be dry or fresh, sour or sweet.

Fruits Change Over Time

Fruits weren’t always like the ones you see in stores today. A long time ago, bananas used to have big black seeds and not much fruit to eat. Over time, people learned how to farm them, giving us sweet and tasty bananas.
